SIH × ISRO problem statement

Sentinel · Burn-in Intelligence

Burn-in screening for space-grade electronics. Every part is compared with its own lot instead of a fixed datasheet line, so parts drifting too fast get pulled at 24 h instead of spending another 144 h in the chamber.

  • anomaly detector: robust z-scores + an isolation forest, with plain-English flags
  • drift predictor checked against NASA's real MOSFET ageing data
  • camera inspection you control with hand gestures (MediaPipe)
